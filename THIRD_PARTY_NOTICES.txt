Changes made by this project: selected the heart wall from version 3.0 and the chamber
cavities, pulmonary vessels, coronary arteries, cardiac veins and arch branches from
version 4.0; moved the 4.0 meshes up by 10 mm to meet the 3.0 heart wall; cut vessels
near the heart; reduced polygon counts; split the heart wall into chambers by the
nearest cavity; converted coordinates to the viewer's axes; derived the positions of the
explanatory conduction-system overlay from the 4.0 cavities, valves, papillary muscles,
superior vena cava and coronary sinus (BodyParts3D itself has no conduction-system meshes).
