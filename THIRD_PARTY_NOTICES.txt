Changes made by this project:
- Data used. From version 3.0: the heart wall (FMA7274), the three aorta parts (FMA3736,
  FMA3768, FMA3784) and the vena cavae (FMA4720, FMA10951). From version 4.0: the pulmonary
  trunk and arteries, the pulmonary veins, the coronary arteries and cardiac veins, the three
  branches of the aortic arch, 11 valve leaflets and 5 papillary muscles (the last two groups
  are shown in the interior view). The 4.0 chamber cavities, superior vena cava, coronary sinus
  and atrial walls are used only for calculations and are not shown.
- Processing. Moved the 4.0 meshes up by 10 mm to meet the 3.0 heart wall; cut the vessels near
  the heart; reduced polygon counts (mitral and tricuspid leaflets to 30 %, the other valves,
  papillary muscles and vessels to 50 % or less); split the heart wall into the four chambers by
  the nearest 4.0 cavity; converted coordinates to the viewer's axes and to a quantised binary
  format.
- Display. In the interior view the meshes are cut by a plane; the heart wall gets a flat
  cut face and the vessels are shown as open shells. Colours are for identification only.
- Added by this project, not part of BodyParts3D: the positions of the explanatory
  conduction-system overlay, derived from the 4.0 cavities, valves, papillary muscles,
  superior vena cava, coronary sinus and atrial walls (BodyParts3D itself has no
  conduction-system meshes). They are estimates and not anatomical data.
